    Help and support

    Service status and recovery

    Check whether the booking service is responding. This does not expose internal system logs.

    Service status has not been checked yet.

    If something goes wrong
    1. Check service status, then retry when the service is responding.
    2. For a failed booking, check My bookings before submitting again to avoid duplicates.
    3. If your session expired, return to sign in and log back in; your account and saved bookings are stored on the service.
    4. If the issue continues, send a report below. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.

    Report a problem

    Your report is saved for the NEXORA administrators and emailed to the hotel when email delivery is configured. A recent failed request reference is attached when available.

    How do I cancel a booking?

    Open My bookings, choose Manage, then cancel the reservation. You can cancel while its status is Pending, Processing, or Approved.

    When am I charged?

    No payment is collected when you submit a reservation. Requests are approved automatically when a room or parking space is available; otherwise, staff can review them. Cash and bank transfer are payment preferences only. This app does not process payments.

    How do I get into a car park?

    After your reservation is approved, open its active QR pass from My bookings and present it to facility staff for verification. Automated gate access is not integrated.
